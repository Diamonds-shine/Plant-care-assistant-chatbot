Plant Care Tips

Six everyday habits that prevent most houseplant problems, whatever you grow.

Water Wisely

Check the soil before watering. Water thoroughly when the plant's needs indicate it is time, and allow excess water to drain.

Follow the Light

Match your plant to its light conditions. Bright indirect light suits many houseplants, while some species tolerate lower light.

Choose the Right Soil

Use a potting mix appropriate for your plant and a container that drains properly.

Watch the Leaves

Yellowing, spots, curling, and drooping can indicate stress. Check soil moisture, light, pests, and recent environmental changes.

Prevent Pests

Inspect leaves and stems regularly. Isolate affected plants and identify the pest before choosing a treatment.

Repot When Needed

Consider repotting when roots crowd the pot, water drains unusually quickly, or growth is restricted.

About GreenBuddy

GreenBuddy is an educational NLP mini-project that demonstrates how natural language processing can help users find relevant answers to common plant-care questions. It uses text preprocessing, TF-IDF vectorization, cosine similarity, and a predefined knowledge base to identify the most relevant response.

Retrieval, not generation

GreenBuddy does not write new answers or use a large language model. It only returns answers that already exist in its knowledge base, and says so when it cannot find a good match.

Data sources

FloraDB Houseplant Care & Pet-Toxicity dataset (free sample): 100 houseplants with light, watering, humidity and ASPCA pet-toxicity data, from Hugging Face and Kaggle (CC BY-NC 4.0). A script turns each plant into question-and-answer entries, alongside a small hand-written set for general problems.

How a question travels

  1. You ask a questionTyped on this page and sent to the Flask API.
  2. 01Understand the QuestionNormalize and tokenize the input, remove filler words and reduce words to their stems (NLTK).
  3. 02Find the Closest MatchTurn it into a TF-IDF vector and compare it with every stored question using cosine similarity.
  4. 03Return Helpful GuidanceAnswer if the question is plant-related and the score is high enough; otherwise reply with a fallback.
  5. The answer appears in chatWith a “How did I find this?” panel showing the score.
